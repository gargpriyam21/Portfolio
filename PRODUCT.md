# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers and engineers in the AI industry. They often skim in under a minute, and often on a phone, deciding whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio that presents Priyam Garg as a software engineer who builds production AI agents (AWS Agentic AI, Seattle, 4+ years of industry experience). Success: the visitor understands who he is and what he builds within seconds, and reaches out through LinkedIn or the contact form.

## Positioning
The engineering that makes AI agents dependable at enterprise scale: reliability, recovery from failure, speed and cost. He is a generic software engineer working on AI engineering, not a mainframe or legacy specialist.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static single page on GitHub Pages (repository gargpriyam21/Portfolio, base path /Portfolio/). No backend.
- All content in src/data/profile.js; empty fields hide their element.
- Contact form posts to a form service (Formspree) once its URL is configured; hidden until then.
- Resume download appears once a resume file exists (pending).
- Photos and logos are dropped into src/assets by file name.

## Brand Commitments
- Never show an email address or phone number.
- Work numbers stay generic and rounded. No customer names, internal package or code names, ticket IDs, colleague names or security details. IBM i support, Java language packs and the Fargate move are not mentioned.
- Model wording: "Claude on Amazon Bedrock", no version.
- Keep the ink-navy and coral look and the one-line Seattle skyline; he likes both.
- Use other portfolios only as inspiration; never copy their styles.

## Evidence on Hand
- AWS outcomes, generic: hours to minutes on large documentation runs, 75% fewer LLM calls on a consolidated step, multi-day jobs that resume after failure, larger codebases supported.
- Four publications (2018 to 2020, Elsevier and Springer venues) with links.
- Six academic projects with GitHub repositories; one demo video.
- Real logos for every employer and school (src/assets/logos).
- Not available, never fabricate: a public AI project or repository, testimonials, awards beyond school honours, a resume file (pending), photos (pending, user will add).

## Product Principles
1. Prove, don't claim: show agent engineering working, labelled as a simplified replay where data is made up.
2. Lead with the AI engineering; earlier history supports it and is told once.
3. Skimmable in 30 seconds on a phone.
4. Generic at the edges: nothing that needs an employer's sign-off.
